📖 Underwriting Policy, Risk Models & Mathematical Formulas

Comprehensive documentation of risk parameters, factor derivations, and scoring equations

Section 1 of 4

Section 1: Mandatory Qualification Rules & Input Validation Bounds

1.1 Age Qualification Equation:

The borrower's age is computed from input date of birth relative to current evaluation date:

Age = (Date_current - DOB) / 365.25 ≥ 21

Violation Result: Hard rejection logged (Age < 21).

1.2 PAN Checksum Validation:

Primary identifier validation enforces the 10-character alphanumeric Indian PAN pattern:

Pattern: ^[A-Z]{5}[0-9]{4}[A-Z]{1}$

Positions 1-3: Sequential, 4th: Holder Type ('P'=Individual), 5th: Name Initial.

1.3 Minimum CIBIL Score Thresholds:

Credit score bounds vary dynamically based on product collateralization:

  • Unsecured Loans (Personal / Business): Score ≥ 650
  • Secured Loans (Home / Auto / Education): Score ≥ 620
1.4 Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR):

Calculates total existing debt burden relative to monthly income:

FOIR = Monthly Debts / (Annual Income / 12) ≤ 0.50 (50%)

Section 2: Bank Risk Factor Coefficients & Asset Collateralization

The Bank Risk Factor Coefficient acts as a penalty weight applied against loan collateral asset quality:

Loan Category Underlying Collateral Risk Factor Risk Impact Rationale
Home Loan Real Estate Property Hypothecation 0.85x Lowest risk profile; backed by real estate assets.
Education Loan Future Earning Potential 1.00x Moderate risk; based on future placement potential.
Auto Loan Vehicle Hypothecation 1.10x Low-Moderate risk; secured by depreciating moveable assets.
Small Business Loan Unsecured Working Capital 1.25x High risk profile; subject to commercial cashflow fluctuations.
Personal Loan Unsecured (Nil Collateral) 1.35x Highest risk profile; zero physical collateral recovery mechanism.

Section 3: Composite Scoring Equation

Personal Loan Underwriting Score Formula:
Score = (CIBIL * 0.60) + ((1.0 / LTI) * 100.0) - (BankRiskFactor * 20.0)
Calculation Example (INR ₹):

Input Parameters: Annual Income = ₹1,200,000 | Monthly Debts = ₹10,000 | Requested Principal = ₹200,000 | CIBIL Score = 750 | Selected Product = Personal Loan (Risk Factor = 1.35)

1. LTI Ratio = 200,000 / 1,200,000 = 0.1667
2. FOIR Ratio = (10,000 * 12) / 1,200,000 = 0.10 (10%)
3. Raw Score = (750 * 0.6) + ((1.0 / 0.1667) * 100) - (1.35 * 20.0) = 450 + 600 - 27 = 1023.0
4. Policy Checks: CIBIL ≥ 650 (PASS), FOIR ≤ 0.50 (PASS), LTI ≤ 4.0 (PASS) → APPROVED

Section 4: Underwriting Decision Matrix & Action Boundaries

✅ APPROVED

Zero policy violations recorded across CIBIL, FOIR, and LTI metrics.

Action: Instant digital sanction letter generation.
⚠️ CONDITIONALLY APPROVED

Exactly one policy check failed, but applicant CIBIL score ≥ 620.

Action: Requires manual underwriter approval.
❌ REJECTED

Multiple policy threshold failures or CIBIL score below minimum requirement.

Action: Application declined; rejection reasons appended.
