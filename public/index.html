📌 Basic Loan Guidelines & Eligibility Rules

  • Age Qualification: Applicant must be between 21 and 60 years old.
  • CIBIL Requirement: Minimum credit score of 620+ for secured loans (Home/Auto) and 650+ for Personal Loans.
  • Fixed Obligations Ratio (FOIR): Total monthly debt obligations must not exceed 45% to 55% of gross monthly income.
  • KYC Verification: Valid PAN and active mobile number linked to Aadhaar are mandatory.
  • Processing Time: Standard applications are evaluated within 24 hours.
